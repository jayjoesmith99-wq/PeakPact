# PeakPact Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-11

PeakPact collects and processes data required to deliver identity transformation, mission tracking, and accountability features.

## Data We Collect
- Account identifiers (email, profile metadata)
- Mission activity, streaks, and progression
- Squad interactions and shared challenge activity
- Purchase and entitlement status
- Device diagnostics required for reliability and support

## How We Use Data
- Operate core application features
- Restore sessions and user progress
- Provide analytics and feature quality improvements
- Deliver subscription and entitlement functionality

## Data Sharing
PeakPact does not sell personal data. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate the application, such as authentication, database, and payment infrastructure.

## Data Retention
Data is retained while an account is active and for a reasonable period needed for legal, security, or fraud prevention obligations.

## Security
PeakPact applies technical and organizational safeguards, including validated inputs, protected sessions, and secure transport.

## Your Rights
Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data.
