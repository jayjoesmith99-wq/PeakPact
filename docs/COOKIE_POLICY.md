## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, improve performance, and provide analytics. Similar technologies such as local storage, device identifiers, and tracking pixels may also be used.

## 3. How We Use Cookies

PeakPact uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

### a. Essential Cookies

Required for the Service to function properly. These cookies enable core features such as security, account login, and navigation.

You cannot disable essential cookies.

### b. Performance & Analytics Cookies

Used to understand how users interact with the Service, improve functionality, and diagnose issues.

Examples include:

- Usage statistics
- Crash reports
- Feature performance metrics

### c. Preference Cookies

Store your settings and choices, such as language, theme, or saved preferences.

### d. Third-Party Cookies
